# Inkling-2-Mini long context on Apple Silicon MLX: what to adopt (checked 2026-11-21 ~23:30 CET)

Read-only sweep of GitHub repos, READMEs, PR bodies and X posts, done before we picked a base (2026-11-21).
All third-party numbers below are the authors' claims. None have been reproduced on Studio 1 (M4 Max, 64 GB, 51.84 GiB Metal cap).
The sweep is partial. GitHub search hit its rate limit, and the last X and exo searches were cut off.

Our baseline (stock mlx-vlm on Studio 1, bf16 KV): 131k at 402 tok/s prefill; 262k at 256 tok/s, 35.4 GiB peak; 524k at 129 tok/s (67.5 min), 46.8 GiB peak, needle found; 786k runs out of Metal memory.

## What nobody has published yet (our gap, as far as this sweep found)
1. **512k–1M on Apple Silicon with a needle check.** Published Mac maximums are 262k (razorback16), about 131k (betizzel), and 384k (anecdote). Our stock 524k needle on a 64 GB M4 Max is already past every Mac number we found.
2. **Long-context prefill throughput on a Mac with the fused rel-bias/sink kernel.** Every kernel benchmark stops at 16k (#2086), 51k/125k (betizzel), or 98k (oMLX).
3. **1M on a single 64 GB Mac.** Estimate, not measured: bf16 KV is 32 GiB plus 18.2–18.8 GiB of weights, about 50–51 GiB, which is right at the 51.84 GiB cap and very likely won't fit. FP8 or 8-bit KV on the 8 global caches is about 16 GiB, about 35 GiB total, which should fit. The 8-bit option is a quality trade-off and needs the needle check plus an eval.
4. **A 2× Mac Studio TB5 split for Inkling.** #2086 says pipeline parallel does not work (the looped layers); TP works. No published Mac TP long-context run.
5. A long-context **prefix-cache** reuse number above 125k (betizzel shows 125k only).

## What we took
We adopted #1 (betizzel's fork, which carries the #2 `relative_attention` kernel) as the base, kept bf16 KV, and built exact memory, decode and prefill patches on top. See the top-level README for measured results.
